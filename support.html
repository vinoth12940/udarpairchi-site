Common questions

The Chat tab says it needs Apple Intelligence. Chat needs a device that supports Apple Intelligence, with it turned on in Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri, and iOS 26 or later. Reading meal photos needs iOS 27. Workouts and the food log work on every supported device.

Where do the calorie numbers come from? From a food database in the app, built from USDA FoodData Central, with a few typical-recipe dishes marked as estimates. Photo amounts are estimates too; tap any food to adjust it.

How do I change my training days? Open the Progress tab and edit your training week.

How do I delete my data? Delete a meal from the Food log, clear the chat with the new-chat button, or delete the app to remove everything.
