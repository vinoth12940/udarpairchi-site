Common questions

Does Chat work without Apple Intelligence? Yes. Chat works on all iOS 17+ devices using the built-in Smart Engine, or you can tap the model pill at the top of Chat to download the optional on-device LFM3-VL-2B model (~1.25 GB) for offline meal-photo vision and chat. On supported devices with Apple Intelligence enabled (iOS 26+ for chat, iOS 27+ for meal photos), you can switch between Apple Intelligence, LFM3-VL-2B, and Smart Engine anytime.

Where do the calorie numbers come from? From an offline database built into the app from USDA FoodData Central (over 13,600 foods), with typical-recipe dishes marked as estimates. Photo amounts are estimates too; tap any food row to adjust portions or swap an item.

How do I switch between Gym and Home workouts or find mobility routines? Open Today or Program and switch between Gym and Home at the top. Guided Warm-up and Cooldown routines sit at the bottom of the Program tab and on rest days.

How does Apple Health work in Udarpairchi? Open Today or Progress and tap Connect Apple Health. Udarpairchi reads and displays steps, walking and running distance, active calories, stand hours and minutes, sleep, resting heart rate, body weight, water intake, menstrual cycle flow, and workouts on your device, and can save completed workouts, weight, hydration, and cycle days back to Apple Health.

How do I set stand-up and hydration reminders or change training days? Open the Progress tab to toggle Stand & water alerts, edit your training week, log weight, or manage your personal cycle log.

How do I delete my data? Delete a meal or water entry from the Food log, clear weight or cycle entries from Progress, clear the chat with the new-chat button, or delete the app to remove local app data.
