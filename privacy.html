Udarpairchi

Privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026. This policy covers the Udarpairchi app for iPhone and iPad.

In short: Udarpairchi doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your training days, completed workouts, in-progress workout and voice-cue setting.
  • Your food log: meals, amounts, water intake, calorie goal and any meal photos you choose to add.
  • Your local body weight history, personal cycle log (optional symptoms and notes), and reminder schedule.
  • Your chat messages and details you ask the chat to remember, which you can view and delete in the app.

This data isn't sent to us or to anyone else. Your device's own backup settings (for example iCloud Backup) may include it. Deleting the app removes it from the device.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Udarpairchi requests on-device read access for steps, walking and running distance, active energy burned, Apple Stand hours and stand time, sleep analysis, resting heart rate, body mass (weight), dietary water, menstrual flow, and workouts, plus write access to save workouts, active energy, body weight, water intake, and menstrual flow entries.

All Apple Health data is read and displayed strictly on your device to power your Progress views, widgets, and optional on-device health chat context. Udarpairchi never sends Apple Health data to external servers, and never uses, sells, or shares HealthKit data for advertising, marketing, data brokering, or AI training.

Notifications

If you turn on stand-up or hydration reminders, Udarpairchi schedules local iOS notifications directly on your device. No remote push servers or device tokens are used.

On-device AI models (Apple Intelligence & LFM3-VL-2B)

The chat and meal-photo features run completely on your device using Apple's on-device Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence), the built-in Smart Engine, or an optional open-weight local model (LFM3-VL-2B) that you can choose to download once from Hugging Face directly to your device. Your messages and meal photos are processed locally on your device and are never sent to us or any third-party AI server. Downloaded model files are stored locally (excluded from iCloud Backup) and can be deleted anytime in the model picker. See Apple's privacy policy for how Apple Intelligence handles data.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a meal photo. The photo picker gives the app only the pictures you select.

Nutrition data

Calorie and nutrient values come from a food database built into the app from USDA FoodData Central (public domain). Looking up foods happens on your device.

Children

Udarpairchi isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: see the support page.
