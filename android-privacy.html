Privacy policy for Android

Last updated November 22, 2026. This policy covers the Udarpairchi app for Android phones and tablets. The iPhone and iPad app has its own policy.

In short: Udarpairchi doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics, no cloud sync and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your training days, completed workouts, workout history, exercise weights, in-progress workout and voice-cue setting.
  • Your food log: meals, amounts, water intake, calorie goal and any meal photos you choose to add.
  • Your local body weight history, personal cycle & symptom log (strictly optional wellness notes), and reminder schedule.
  • Your chat messages and details you ask the chat to remember, which you can view and delete in the app.

This data isn't sent to us or to anyone else. Android's backup to your Google account may include app preferences if you have backup turned on; the downloaded AI model file is never backed up. Uninstalling the app removes all local data from the device.

Health Connect

If you choose to connect Android Health Connect, Udarpairchi requests on-device read access for steps, distance, active calories burned, sleep sessions and stages, resting heart rate, body weight, hydration (water), menstrual flow, and exercise sessions, plus write access to save workouts, active calories, body weight, water intake, and menstrual flow entries.

All Health Connect data is read and displayed strictly on your device to power your Today, Food log, Progress, Home Screen widget, and optional on-device chat context (cycle day context is off by default and opt-in). Udarpairchi never transmits Health Connect data off your device, and never uses, sells, or shares Health Connect data for advertising, marketing, data brokering, or AI training. You can revoke any Health Connect permission at any time in Android Settings → Health Connect.

Notifications

If you turn on stand-up or hydration reminders, or during active workout rest timers, Udarpairchi posts local Android notifications directly on your device. No remote push servers or device tokens are used.

On-device AI chat & meal photos (Smart Engine & Gemma 4 E2B)

Chat works immediately offline with Udarpairchi's built-in Smart Engine (0 MB download). If you also choose to download Google's open Gemma 4 E2B model (about 2.6 GB) for on-device multimodal chat and meal-photo vision, the app downloads the SHA-256 verified file once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) using Android's DownloadManager. Like any file download, that request reveals your device's IP address to Hugging Face; no messages, photos, health data or other personal data are sent. After that, all messages and photos are processed locally on your device. You can remove the model anytime from the Chat model picker or Progress tab.

Each chat reply has a Report button. Reporting hides the reply on your phone. If you also choose to email the report, your email app sends that reply's text to us, and we use it only to improve the app.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a meal photo, through your phone's camera app. The Android photo picker gives the app only the pictures you select. The app doesn't request broad access to your photo library or camera.

Nutrition data

Calorie and nutrient values come from a 13,679-food database built into the app from USDA FoodData Central (public domain) plus typical-recipe estimates. All lookups happen on your device.

Children

Udarpairchi isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.
