Privacy policy for Android

Last updated September 25, 2026. This policy covers the Udarpairchi app for Android phones and tablets. The iPhone and iPad app has its own policy.

In short: Udarpairchi doesn't collect your data. There's no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your training days, completed workouts, in-progress workout and voice-cue setting.
  • Your food log: meals, amounts, calorie goal and any meal photos you choose to add.
  • Your chat messages and details you ask the chat to remember, which you can view and delete in the app.

This data isn't sent to us or to anyone else. Android's backup to your Google account may include it if you have backup turned on; the chat model file is never backed up. Uninstalling the app removes it from the device.

Chat and meal photos (Gemma, on your phone)

The chat and meal-photo features use Google's open Gemma model, which runs entirely on your phone. When you choose to set up chat, the app downloads the model file (about 2.6 GB) once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) using Android's download service. Like any download, that request reveals your device's IP address to Hugging Face; no messages, photos or other personal data are sent. After that, your messages and photos are processed on your phone and aren't sent to us or anyone else. You can remove the model from the Progress tab.

Each chat reply has a Report button. Reporting hides the reply on your phone. If you also choose to email the report, your email app sends that reply's text to us, and we use it only to improve the chat.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a meal photo, through your phone's camera app. The Android photo picker gives the app only the pictures you select. The app doesn't request access to your photo library or camera.

Nutrition data

Calorie and nutrient values come from a food database built into the app from USDA FoodData Central (public domain). Looking up foods happens on your device.

Permissions

Internet and network state (only to download the chat model) and vibration (for workout cues). No location, contacts, health or activity permissions.

Children

Udarpairchi isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone.
